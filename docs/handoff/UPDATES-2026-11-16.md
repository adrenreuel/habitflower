# HabitFlowers: UI updates since your last notes (Nov 16, 2026)

The design canvas is the source of truth. Board names are in brackets. Keep the existing rules: no em dashes in copy, usernames only, "check in" (never "water"), say "HabitFlowers".

## 1. Visual style

- **Grain:** add a subtle noise texture over the app background (base `#1A0C1D`, noise at about 7% opacity). Cards sit on top without grain so they still stand out.
- **Squircle corners:** use continuous corners on cards and sheets (`borderCurve: 'continuous'`).
- **Habit page tint:** each habit page has a soft gradient of its flower colour at the top, fading into the background.
- **Bottom nav** [Habits, Tasks, Friends, Activity, empty states]:
  - No border line on top and no ring around the middle button.
  - Background `#2F1F36` with a soft shadow above it (`0 -10px 28px rgba(0,0,0,0.38)`).
  - The add button is a green pill (52x40, radius 20, `#74D447`) sitting inside the bar, lined up with the other tabs. It no longer pokes up above the bar.

## 2. Habits screen

- **Chips:** only the bloom state gets a chip on habit cards. No chips for sprout, bud or wilted.
- **Away mode** [Home: away mode]:
  - When away mode is on, Home shows a full-screen overlay. It dims and blurs the habit list, and the habit cards can't be tapped.
  - The header (date, title, settings gear) and the bottom nav stay above the overlay and stay usable.
  - Centred content, with no card behind it:
    - Beach umbrella icon.
    - "You're away" in Young Serif.
    - "Your HabitFlowers won't wilt, and reminders are off until you're back."
    - **I'm back** button, which turns away mode off.
  - The old away banner on Home is removed.

## 3. Habit page

- **Hero:**
  - No square tile around the flower. It's a 156px flower that gently sways (rotate between -2° and 2° over 4.5s, ease-in-out, pivot near the stem base).
  - The soil oval does **not** sway. Draw it as a separate static shape behind the flower, and draw the flower without its own soil. The canvas Flower component has a `soil` on/off prop for this.
  - Revive (dormant) pages don't sway.
- **History card:**
  - Replaces the old This week, Stats and calendar sections.
  - One tappable card with the week grid, a divider and two inline stats.
  - Tapping it opens a **History sheet** with:
    - A 2x2 stats grid.
    - Month navigation, with next disabled on the current month.
    - The calendar with its legend.
- **Settings card:** every habit page shows a **Shared with** row (two check-in windows and gym pages were missing it).
- **Removed:** the "Photo source: Camera only" row on photo habits.

## 4. Growth moments [row 3]

Full-screen celebration when a flower changes state.

- **Layout:**
  - Background `#140918`.
  - Slowly rotating light rays and a soft radial glow behind the flower.
  - Falling confetti in the flower's colour only.
  - No tile and no sparkles. The flower is 200px and loops old state → new state.
  - Headline in Young Serif, 34px.
- **Order, top to bottom:**
  1. Flower.
  2. Headline.
  3. Body.
  4. Growth track: a stepper pill. Past stages are filled checks, the current stage is a tinted 44px circle with no glow, and future stages are grey, faded flower previews.
  5. Button, in a footer at the bottom.
- **Motion:**
  - The headline, body, track and button fade in **once** and never fade back out.
  - Only the flower, rays, glow and confetti loop.

| Moment | Headline | Body | Button |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed → bud | It budded | Your Lavender grew a bud. Check in again tomorrow and it blooms. | Nice |
| Bud → bloom | It bloomed! | | Amazing! |
| 30-day milestone | 30 days in bloom | | Keep it going |
| Wilted → bloom | Perked back up | | Nice |
| Dormant → bud | A bud again | | Nice |
| Dried sprout → sprout | Growing again | Your Rose is growing again. Check in again tomorrow and it buds. | Nice |

- **Flower rule reminder:** a sprout that dries up revives as a sprout.

## 5. New and edit habit

- **Flower picker** [New habit, Edit habit]:
  - The "Your flower" grid in the form is gone.
  - The flower now sits centred at the top of the form, above the name field. It's a 104px tinted tile with an 84px flower, and a white **Change** pill (swap icon) on its bottom edge.
  - Tapping it opens the **Pick a flower** sheet:
    - **Preview card**, tinted with the selected flower's colour. It shows the flower swaying on still soil, its name in Young Serif, and its sprout → bud → bloom stages in a row.
    - **Grid**, 4 columns, scrolling inside the sheet so it scales to any number of flowers. The selected flower has a ring in its own colour. Flowers already used by another habit get a small muted **Used** tag. This is a hint only, never blocked.
    - "How to grow?" link in the sheet header.
    - Button: **Pick {Flower}**. Tapping outside the sheet cancels.
  - A new habit defaults to a flower the user isn't using yet.
- **History sharing:**
  - In the Share progress sheet, add **Full history:** `Nobody` / `Close friends` / `Everyone`. The default is **Nobody**.
  - Anyone who can see the habit but isn't on the chosen list sees **this week only**: the flower, the streak and the current week.
  - Hint lines:
    - **Nobody:** "Friends see your flower, streak and this week. Older days stay private."
    - **Close friends:** "{names} see everything. Everyone else sees this week."
    - **Everyone:** "Everyone above can open the calendar and stats, back to day one."
  - The choice is hidden when the habit is shared with nobody.
  - The Sharing card gets a **History** row showing "This week", "Close friends", "Full history" or "Only you".
- **Name lists:** this applies to the History hint and the check-in photos hint.
  - 1 friend: "@a"
  - 2 friends: "@a and @b"
  - 3 friends: "@a, @b and @c"
  - 4 or more: "@a, @b and N others"

## 6. Editing rules (Edit habit)

- **How you check in is locked** after creation.
  - Show it as a read-only row with a lock icon.
  - Under it: "To check in another way, start a new habit." The link opens New habit pre-filled.
  - The settings for that type (list items, count, photos, Health goal) stay editable.
- **Schedule changes start tomorrow.** Show "Changes start tomorrow. Past days stay as they were." (blue info note with a clock icon) after the user changes the schedule.
- **Goal, count, windows, list items and Health goal** apply from today onward.
- **Past days always keep the rules they were judged by.** Never recalculate past streaks or misses.
- **Group habits:**
  - Only the creator can change shared rules (check-in type, schedule, windows). Members get an Activity notice when the rules change.
  - Members see a note at the top: "@{creator} sets the shared rules. You can change your own reminders and sharing."
  - For members, How you check in, Schedule, Check-in window and the flower are disabled. Reminders and Sharing stay editable.
  - For members, **Delete habit** becomes **Leave group** ("The flower keeps growing for everyone else").

## 7. Friends and Activity

- **Requests moved:** friend requests and group invites moved from Friends to Activity. They're in a **Requests** section at the top, followed by New and Earlier.
- **Friends heading:** the "HabitFlowers" heading on Friends is removed.
- **Friend's flowers page:** every habit card is tappable and opens a sheet.
  - **Full history shared with you:** streak and completion stats, plus the month calendar in the flower's colour.
  - **This week only:** the week grid plus a lock row, "@{user} only shares this week for this habit."
- **Copy:** use "Check mail" instead of "Read 20 pages" as a sample habit.

## 8. Empty states

- **Activity:** a lavender bud with a moon and stars. Copy: "Reminders, nudges and reactions show up here."
- **Habits:** seed-packet suggestion cards.
  - Each card is tinted with its flower colour, has the habit name in Young Serif and a meta line, and a large flower in the corner.
  - The section is "Suggestions", and the gear button goes to Settings.
- **Paused habits (new):** an empty glass dome. Heading "Nothing paused". Body "Need a break from a habit? Pause it from its Edit screen."

## 9. App icon

- **Use the white version for now:** `icon/habitflowers-icon-1024.png` (1024x1024, square, no rounded corners or transparency, so iOS can apply its own mask). Source vector: `icon/habitflowers-icon.svg`.
- **Design:** half a sunflower rising from the bottom edge, with a bold green checkmark (`#4FA82A`) centred above it. "Flower" plus "habit".
- **Petals:**
  - 6 rounded, overlapping petals in `#FFC94F`, `#FFC23D`, `#FFB72B` and `#FFB31F`.
  - They use a multiply blend, so the intersections are darker.
- **Centre:** brown `#5B3415`, on a white background.
- **Sizes:** generate the iOS sizes from the 1024px PNG.
